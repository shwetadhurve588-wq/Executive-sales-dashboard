
# 📊 Executive E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive two-page Power BI report designed to track high-level sales KPIs and deliver strategic business recommendations to address profit erosion caused by promotional discounting.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Key Findings

* **Profit Leak Identified:** The **Furniture** category generates over 30% of sales volume but yields a net profit margin under 2.5% due to aggressive promotional discounting exceeding 15%.
* **Core Revenue Driver:** The **Technology** category generates 40% of overall revenue and over 60% of net profits, with Copiers and Phones maintaining the highest overall margins.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Cap promotional discounts on Furniture at a maximum of 10% and shift promotional marketing budget toward high-margin Technology categories.
